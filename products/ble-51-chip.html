01

LE5110系列主要特性

LE5110系列产品是高性能的工业级低功耗BLE SoC，支持蓝牙5.1全协议栈，优秀的静电放电（ESD） 抗扰度性能，HBMM≥8KV;，可以广泛应用于工业控制、物品追踪、高精度室内定位等场景。

  • 工业级BLE SoC，支持BLE 5.1 全协议栈；
  • 内置高速存储器FLASH512kB ，64kB SRAM；
  • 优秀的静电放电（ ESD） 抗扰度性能，HBM≥8KV;
  • 包含32-bit MCU内核，时钟频率可调，最高可达64MHz；
  • RF射频性能指标； 最高-105dBm的RX灵敏度 最大+13dBm的TX功率 具备117dBm链路增益，确保良好的信号覆盖范围
  • 超低功耗设计，在SRAM和RTC运行下，待机电流低至1.1μA；
  • 支持L2CAP、GAP、GATT、SMP及SIG组织定义的各类Profile；
  • 集成丰富外设，包括互补PWM输出、电容式触摸接口、高精度ADC、AES/DES/ECC安全单元、高速串口等；

技术参数

BLE RF Transceiver

  • 2.4GHz RF transceiver for BLE 5.1;
  • 125K/500K/1M/2M bps data rate;
  • -105dBm RX sensitivity @BLE 125Kbps;
  • Programmable TX power up to +13dBm;
  • Single-ended antenna output;

BLE Protocol

  • BLE 5.1 with AoA / AoD feature;
  • SIG BLE MESH / Proprietary BLE MESH;

Low Power

  • Active-Mode RX: 4.5mA @3.3V;
  • Active-Mode TX: 4.3mA @3.3V 0dBm;
  • Standby: 1.1µA (RTC running and RAM retention);
  • Shutdown: 700nA (Wake up on GPIO events);

Microcontroller

  • Up to 64MHz clock speed;
  • 512kB Flash;
  • 64kB SRAM;

Peripherals

  • Configurable GPIOs;
  • 4 timers (with 1 advanced timer);
  • Complementary pwm output;
  • IWDT / WWDT;
  • Real timer counter (RTC);
  • I2C / SPI / UART;
  • 8 channel 12bit ADC;
  • 12 channel DMA;
  • Integrated temperature sensor;
  • On-chip DC-DC, LDO, POR, PDR;
  • AES/DES, ECC, TRNG;
